  layout="stacked"
  image={pathsPanel}
  alt="The Paths tab, showing the starting point and an expanded path panel with its name, target position, heading mode, control points, and the Insert after buttons."

Click the field to add a path, then drag the points to shape the curve.

Choose how the robot faces on each path.

[Path Editing](/turtle-tracer/path-editing/)

</HomeSection>

<HomeSection
  title="Watch it drive"
  layout="stacked"
  image={chainedCorner}
  alt="The field as the robot drives a route, with the view following the robot. Dashed lines show where it carries on past a chained corner, swings out, and steers back onto the next path."

    height: 1254,

Press `Space` and the robot drives your route, timed with your robot's speed and braking.

Chained paths swing wide at the join, the way Pedro drives them.

[Simulation & Playback](/turtle-tracer/simulation/)

</HomeSection>

<HomeSection
  title="Catch problems early"
  layout="stacked"
  image={validation}
  alt="Validation markers on the field: a red stretch where the path runs into an obstacle, a yellow marker at a chained corner, and a magenta marker at a zero-length segment."
>

Validation marks paths that hit an obstacle or leave the field.

The optimizer can find a faster way around.

[Path Validation](/turtle-tracer/validation/) and [Path Optimization](/turtle-tracer/optimization/)

</HomeSection>

<HomeSection
  title="Export the code"
  layout="stacked"
  frame={false}
  large

Java or Kotlin, as a plain OpMode or for SolversLib, NextFTC, or Ivy.

Event markers run your robot's actions at exact points along a path.

[Exporting Code](/turtle-tracer/exporting/)

</HomeSection>

<HomeSection title="See it on the real robot">

TurtleTracerLib sends your robot's position to the app over Wi-Fi, so you can watch it on the field.

Import a log from a run to compare it with your plan.

[Live View](/turtle-tracer-lib/live-view/) and [Telemetry Overlay](/turtle-tracer/telemetry/)

</HomeSection>
